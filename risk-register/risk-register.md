# Cybersecurity Risk Register

## 📌 Overview
This project presents a cybersecurity risk register for a small organization. It identifies key risks, evaluates their likelihood and impact, and outlines mitigation strategies to reduce overall risk exposure.

## 🏢 Business Scenario
The organization is a small business with approximately 20 employees operating in a hybrid environment. It relies on laptops, email systems, cloud storage, and a public-facing website.
